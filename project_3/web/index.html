1.2.3 Denoising Pure Noise

What patterns appear, and why?

When training with noisy images, it still contains information about the original image, hence the model can reconstruct that image. However for denoising from pure noise, the model has no clue as to which digit to produce, resulting in producing the average training image no matter what, which is the minimum of sum of squared distances across all training samples. Hence, whatever pure noise is inputted, the same image is outputted during training, resulting in a constant MSE.

What did you do to compensate for removing the scheduler?

I used a constant learning rate of 1e-3, which is around the learning rate the scheduler would decay to. This provides more stable updates, although it learns more slowly at the beginning.

Final comparison

I tried 3 different approaches:

  1. Increased the number of epochs to 30.

    Epoch: 30, Hidden Dims: 64

    • The final output at Epoch 30 has much more identifiable and clearer digits than the output at Epoch 10
    • Somewhat disconnected, straight lines have become connected and smoother
  2. Increased Hidden Dims

    Epoch: 30, Hidden Dims: 128

    • This took a longer time than training at 64 hidden_dims
    • The results could be argued to be slightly better than the base model, but isn’t a definite difference
  3. Modified Model Architecture (added an extra time scaling component)

    Epoch: 30, Hidden Dims: 64

    • The final approach modified the model architecture by adding an extra FCBlock that scales the second upblock’s output. This allows every scale of the decoder to know t, hoping for the model to have a deeper understanding of acting at certain levels of t.
    • I also scaled by a factor of 1+ fc(t) instead of just fc(t), to guard against the case where an extremely low output of fc(t) (near zero) will suppress the feature.

Conclusion:

  • The final method, adding an extra time scaling component, seemed to have the best output when looking at the clarity of the digit (no noise) and if it is an identifiable digit, out of the three methods.
  • Compared to the base model output at epoch 10, all three methods show improvements.
